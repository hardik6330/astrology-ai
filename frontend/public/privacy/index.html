Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 June 2026

This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We've kept it in plain English. By using Selora you agree to the practices described here.

The short version
  • Your palm photo is never stored — it's used to create your reading, then discarded.
  • We never sell your data, and we don't use it to train AI models.
  • We collect only what's needed to create your readings and run your account.
  • You can request deletion of your account and data at any time.

What we collect

  • Phone number — used to sign you in securely via a one-time code (OTP). It identifies your account.
  • Birth details — your date, time, and place of birth, used to generate your chart and readings.
  • Palm photos — when you use palm reading. See "Palm photos" below.
  • Your readings and chat history — saved to your account so you can return to them.
  • Basic usage & device data — to keep the service secure, reliable, and improving.

Palm photos — never stored

When you scan your palm, the image is used only to create your reading and is then discarded. We do not save the image to our database, and we never use your photo to train anything. We keep only a one-way fingerprint (a hash that can't be turned back into an image) and the text of your reading.

How we use your data

  • To create and show your charts, palm readings, daily guidance, and chat answers.
  • To run your account, credits, and purchases.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To send notifications you've opted into.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • We do not store your palm images.
  • We do not store your payment card details (handled by our payment providers).

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the service — for sign-in/notifications, for processing payments, and for generating AI readings. They process data only to provide their part of the service, under their own security and privacy commitments. Payments are handled by the payment provider; we never see or store your full card details.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (below); we'll remove it except where we're required to keep limited records (for example, transaction records for tax/legal reasons).

Your choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your data — contact us.
  • Turn notifications on or off at any time.
  • Stop using the service and request account deletion.

Children

Selora is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll change the "last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.

Contact
