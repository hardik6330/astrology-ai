Astrology & Palmistry Glossary

Last updated: 20 June 2026 · By the Selora team

Plain-English definitions of the Vedic astrology and palmistry terms you'll meet in a reading — from the ascendant and dasha periods to the life and heart lines. Each term is defined in a single clear sentence.

On this page: ascendant, natal chart, houses, dasha, nakshatra, gochar, ayanamsha, mangal dosha, sade sati, rahu kaal, and the major palm lines and mounts.

Vedic astrology terms

Ascendant (Lagna)
The zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at your birth; it sets the layout of the houses and represents your core self and outlook.
Natal chart (Kundli)
A map of the sky at the exact moment and place of your birth, showing where each planet sat across the twelve houses.
House (Bhava)
One of twelve divisions of the chart, each governing a life area such as self, wealth, career, or relationships.
Dasha
A Vedic system of planetary time periods that indicates which planet rules a given chapter of your life and for how long.
Mahadasha
A major planetary period in the dasha system, lasting several years, during which one planet most strongly shapes your life.
Antardasha
A sub-period within a Mahadasha, ruled by a second planet, that refines the timing and theme of that chapter.
Nakshatra (Birth star)
One of 27 lunar mansions the Moon occupies at birth; it adds finer detail to personality and temperament.
Gochar (Transit)
The current real-time position of the planets in the sky, read against your birth chart to gauge present influences.
Rashi (Moon sign)
The zodiac sign occupied by the Moon at birth; in Vedic astrology it is often treated as the primary sign.
Ayanamsha
The angular offset used to convert the tropical zodiac to the sidereal zodiac that Vedic astrology is based on.
Sidereal zodiac
A zodiac fixed to the actual positions of the stars, used in Vedic astrology, as opposed to the season-based tropical zodiac.
Mangal Dosha
A chart condition caused by Mars placement in certain houses, traditionally considered in relationship compatibility.
Sade Sati
A roughly seven-and-a-half-year period when Saturn transits over and around the natal Moon, associated with testing life lessons.
Rahu Kaal
A recurring inauspicious window during the day, traditionally avoided for starting important new activities.

Palmistry terms

Life line
The palm line curving around the base of the thumb; traditionally linked to vitality and major life changes, not lifespan.
Head line
The palm line running across the middle of the palm; traditionally linked to thinking style and decision-making.
Heart line
The uppermost major palm line; traditionally linked to emotions, relationships, and attachment style.
Fate line
A vertical palm line rising toward the middle finger; traditionally linked to career direction and life path.
Mount of Venus
The fleshy area at the base of the thumb; in palmistry it is associated with warmth, vitality, and affection.
