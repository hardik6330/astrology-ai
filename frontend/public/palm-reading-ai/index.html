AI Palm Reading: Scan Your Palm Online

Last updated: 20 June 2026 · By the Astro AI team

AI palm reading uses computer vision to analyze your palm from a photo — detecting and measuring the major lines (life, head, heart, and fate) and the mounts of the hand, then producing a structured palmistry reading. Astro AI reads a single hand or compares both, reports the measured geometry of your lines rather than invented numbers, and never stores your photo.

How AI palm reading works

  1. Take a clear photo of your open palm in good light, fingers slightly spread.
  2. On-device quality check confirms the hand and lines are visible before anything is uploaded — so you don't waste a reading on a blurry shot.
  3. AI analysis detects and measures the lines and mounts and returns a structured reading, with the real geometry shown as evidence.

Is AI palm reading accurate?

The measurements are real: AI vision reliably detects line length, curvature, and position from a clear photo, and Astro AI shows those measured values instead of inventing them. The interpretation follows traditional palmistry, which is an art rather than a science — but grounding it in measured geometry makes an AI palm reading far more consistent and transparent than a generic "fortune teller" app.

Privacy: your photo is never stored

Astro AI uses your palm image only to create the reading, then discards it — only a one-way hash (to recognise repeat uploads) and the text reading are kept. Your image is never saved to a database or used to train anything.
