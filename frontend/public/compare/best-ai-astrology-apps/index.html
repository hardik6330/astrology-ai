2. Co-Star

A popular Western (tropical) astrology app known for its minimalist design and social features. Good for daily vibes and friend compatibility, but it focuses on Western astrology, has no palm reading, and offers limited chart-grounded conversation.

  • Western only — no Vedic depth (dashas, nakshatras)
  • No AI palm reading
  • Limited interactive chart-aware chat

3. AstroTalk

A marketplace connecting users with human astrologers for paid per-minute consultations. Strong if you specifically want a person, but it's slower, costlier, and answers vary by practitioner — the opposite trade-off to an instant, consistent AI tool.

  • Per-minute pricing adds up
  • Quality varies by astrologer
  • Not AI-native; no instant self-serve readings

4. General AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini)

Great for explaining astrology concepts, but if you ask them to "build my chart," they make up your chart — they guess instead of getting it right, and they forget it between sessions. Use them to learn; use a dedicated app like Selora for an accurate chart and reading.

  • Guesses your chart (inaccurate)
  • No persistent memory of your chart
  • No reliable palm reading
